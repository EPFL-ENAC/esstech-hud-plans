**2. What We Collect**

We designed the App to minimize personal data collection. Specifically:

- **We only store your email address but not name, government ID etc** to use the core functionality of the App.
- **Videos and generated plans** : When you submit a video to be converted into a 2D plan, we save the video and the resulting plan. This is done **solely for research and development purposes** , to improve the App's accuracy and performance while it remains in development.
- **Location data** : If you choose to provide location information (e.g., by tagging a video with a location, or granting location permissions), we save this location data alongside the associated video/plan. Providing location is **optional** and is only stored if you actively supply it.
- **Technical/usage data** : Device type, crash logs.
- Because video content may incidentally capture identifiable people, places, or objects, and because location data is inherently personal data under GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), **we treat videos, plans, and any associated location data as personal data** and protect them accordingly.

**4. Where and How Your Data Is Stored**

- All videos, generated plans, and any associated location data are stored on **secure cloud servers physically located in Switzerland at EPFL** .
- Data is encrypted **in transit** (e.g., TLS 1.2+).
- Access to stored data is restricted to authorized personnel involved in the research and development of the App, under confidentiality obligations.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your videos, plans, or location data with third parties for marketing purposes.
- All processing is done internally on EPFL servers based in Switzerland.

Because Switzerland is recognized by the European Commission as providing an **adequate level of data protection** , transfers of personal data from the EU/EEA to our Swiss servers do not require additional safeguards under GDPR Article 45.

---

**5. How Long We Keep Your Data**

- Videos and generated plans collected for research purposes are retained only for as long as necessary to support the App's development, and in any case for no longer than **1 year** from the date of collection.
- Location data, if provided, is retained for the same period as its associated video/plan and deleted at the same time.
- Once the App exits its development/research phase, or once the retention period expires (whichever comes first), we will delete your data unless you have separately requested earlier deletion (see Section 8) or the law requires longer retention.

**7. Your Rights**

Under the GDPR and the Swiss FADP, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you (i.e., your submitted videos, plans, and location data)
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), including withdrawing consent to the research use of your video/plan/location data at any time
- **Restrict or object** to certain processing
- **Data portability** , where technically feasible, for data you provided directly
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority — in Switzerland, the FDPIC ([www.edoeb.admin.ch](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en)); if you are in the EU/EEA, your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [pointsofhome@groupes.epfl.ch](mailto:pointsofhome@groupes.epfl.ch). We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally one month under GDPR).
